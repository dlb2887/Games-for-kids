# Ariel and the Lost Voice 🧜‍♀️🔱

A six-level story game, designed by a kid who really loves Ariel.

Ursula catches Ariel and steals two things: her voice, and King Triton's trident. While her
voice is gone, every word Ariel says comes out in **Spanish** — so the way to get it back is
to work out what her words mean. Prince Eric comes to help, Ariel's six sisters make a wall
in front of him, and at the end everybody hugs and claps.

Open `index.html` in any browser — double-click it, or play it online at
<https://dlb2887.github.io/Games-for-kids/ariel-lost-voice/>.
It works completely offline. Nothing is downloaded, so it's fine on a plane or in the car.

## The levels

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🏊‍♀️ **The Big Blue Sea** | *Swim.* Collect eight shells while the sisters swim around. Then Ursula turns up. |
| 2 | 🫧 **Ursula's Garden** | *Swim.* Ariel's voice broke into eight Spanish words and floated away. Catch them all. Purple eels bump into you, and that's all they do. |
| 3 | 💡 **Find Your Voice** | *Think.* Ariel says a Spanish word, you pick what it means in English. Every right answer is another piece of her voice. |
| 4 | 🚶‍♀️ **Walk to Prince Eric** | *Walk.* On land a mermaid gets legs, so the controls change: ◀ ▶ to walk and ⤴ to jump over the rocks and crabs. Six hearts along the way. |
| 5 | 👭 **The Sisters Protect Eric** | *Tap.* Ursula throws zaps at Prince Eric. A gold ring shows which sister a zap is heading for — tap her and she blocks it. Block eight. |
| 6 | 🔱 **The Trident** | *Swim.* Ursula is now as big as a mountain. Catch six sparkles to make Ariel glow, then swim up and grab the trident. |

Then the ending: Ursula is gone for good, the trident goes home to King Triton, Ariel's voice
comes back, everybody hugs — and you clap three times. **Go Ariel! Go Ariel!**

## How to play

| | |
|---|---|
| 🏊‍♀️ | **Swimming levels:** drag a finger anywhere in the sea to swim toward it. Arrow keys or WASD work on a computer. |
| 🚶‍♀️ | **Walking level:** hold ◀ or ▶ to walk, tap ⤴ to jump. Arrow keys and the spacebar work too. |
| 👭 | **Sister level:** tap the sister with the gold ring around her. Number keys 1–6 work as well. |
| 🔍 | **When Ariel talks in Spanish,** the 🔍 button tells you what she said, and 🔊 says it again. |
| 💡 | **The hint button never runs out.** First press says the word again, slowly. Second press shows a picture and a clue. Third press starts taking away wrong answers, and after that it just tells you the answer. |
| ⭐ | **Levels stay unlocked** once you finish them, so you can jump back to a favourite from the map. |

## The Spanish

Twelve words, all of them tied to the story: `la voz`, `el mar`, `la hermana`, `el pez`,
`el cangrejo`, `la concha`, `el rey`, `la estrella`, `el amor`, `cantar`, `el barco`,
`la corona`. Eight of them get caught in level 2, and six of those come back as questions in
level 3 — so the quiz only ever asks about words the child has already met and heard.

## For grown-ups

- **Nobody can lose.** There is no health bar, no timer and no game over anywhere in the six
  levels. Bumping an eel, a rock or a crab makes Ariel spin for a second; in the final level a
  zap also knocks one sparkle loose, and it lands right next to her so it can be picked
  straight back up. That's the entire penalty, and it matches the other games here.
- **Wrong answers cost nothing** in level 3. The word wobbles, the game says "try again", and
  the same question stays up until it's right.
- **Spanish voices.** The Spanish is spoken by the browser's speech synthesiser. If it sounds
  like an English speaker reading Spanish, the device has no Spanish voice installed —
  iPad: *Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Spanish*;
  Android: *Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech*; Chrome on a computer has one built in.
  The game still works without it.
- **Sound** is one button — the speaker in the corner mutes both the sound effects and the
  talking, and it remembers the setting.
- **Progress** is saved in `localStorage`, so closing the tab doesn't lose the story.
  *Start the story over* on the home screen resets it.
- **Reading level.** The story text is read aloud automatically, so a pre-reader can play it
  alone; the Spanish words on the bubbles and cards are short enough to sound out.
