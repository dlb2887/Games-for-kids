# Gabby's Christmas Ice Cube Rescue 🧊🎄

A six-level Gabby's Dollhouse story game, written by a five-year-old who loves Gabby's Dollhouse.

On Christmas Eve, Santa comes to visit and brings his best friend from the North Pole, **Pete the
polar bear**. Pete and CatRat climb into Cakey's freezer looking for ice cream and freeze inside a
giant ice cube. The cube slides down the hall and lands *plop* in MerCat's ice-cold pool, and
CatRat really does not like water. Gabby has to rescue them, but her magic cat ears and her rainbow
bracelet have gone missing.

Open `index.html` in any browser (double-click it), or play it online at
<https://dlb2887.github.io/Games-for-kids/gabbys-dollhouse/>.
It works completely offline.

## The levels

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Find the Cat Ears** | *Search.* Tap the presents, the stocking, Pillow Cat, the toy box and other things in Gabby's room to look underneath. The cat ears shrink Gabby down tiny. |
| 2 | **The Rainbow Bracelet** | *Sort.* Baby Box borrowed the bracelet for a craft and it broke. Put the beads back on in rainbow order (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple), twice around. |
| 3 | **To the Store** | *Drive, then count.* Carlita drives to the grocery store: hold ▶ and jump over snowballs and presents. Then find the **three** snorkels hidden among the groceries. |
| 4 | **Spa Science** | *Think.* MerCat says *"Spa science!"* and asks: does ice sink or float? Is hot cocoa warm or cold, and will it melt the ice? What happens to water in the freezer? |
| 5 | **The Icy Pool** | *Swim.* The rainbow bracelet turns Gabby, Pandy and Carlita into mermaids with snorkels. Swim through warm bubbles, dodge the chilly swirls, then swim up to the floating ice cube. |
| 6 | **Melt the Ice** | *Use what you learned.* Drag (or tap) warm things onto the ice cube: cocoa, sunshine, warm spa water, warm cookies. Cold things bounce off. CRACK! CatRat and Pete pop out. |

Then the ending: a Christmas dance party with DJ Catnip. You clap three times, and *Gabby got her
bracelet back, her cat ears back, and CatRat and Pete back. They were all safe and sound, and they
lived happily ever after. The end.*

## What it teaches

- **Ice and water science** is the main lesson, and it runs through the whole story:
  - ice is frozen water, and water freezes when it gets very cold (that's what happened in the freezer);
  - warm things melt ice, and cold things keep it frozen;
  - ice floats, which is why the ice cube bobs on top of the pool.

  Level 4 teaches it and level 6 asks the child to use it.
- **Rainbow order** (level 2): red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple.
- **Counting to three** (level 3): one snorkel each for Gabby, Pandy and Carlita.
- **Kindness**: when Baby Box admits to breaking the bracelet, Gabby says "Everybody makes
  mistakes. Let's fix it together!"

## The characters

Gabby, Pandy Paws, CatRat, Carlita, DJ Catnip, MerCat, Baby Box, Cakey, Pillow Cat, Santa, and
Pete the polar bear (an original character from the author). Every character is a **drawing made
with code** on an HTML canvas, with outlines, blinking eyes and little animations. There are no
emoji and no image files.

## For grown-ups

- **Nobody can lose.** There is no timer, no health bar and no game over. Bumping a snowball or a
  chilly swirl makes you spin for a second. Wrong answers just wobble and get a friendly "try
  again". After a few misses, the right answer starts to sparkle.
- **Everything is read aloud** by the browser's speech synthesiser, and each character has their
  own pitch (Santa is low, CatRat is squeaky), so a child who can't read yet can play alone. Use
  **Say it again** to repeat a line.
- **Hints never run out** in Spa Science. The first hint gives a clue; the second gives the answer.
- **Sound** is one button: the speaker in the corner mutes both the sound effects and the voices,
  and the game remembers the setting.
- **Progress** is saved in `localStorage`. Finished levels stay unlocked on the map, and *Start
  the story over* resets them.
- **Controls:** tap for most levels. To swim, drag a finger toward where you want to go; to drive,
  use the on-screen ◀ ▶ ⤴ buttons. Arrow keys and the space bar also work on a computer.
