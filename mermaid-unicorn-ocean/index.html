Mermaid Unicorn
Ocean

Swim around, collect shells, and pop the cookie bags with your arrows! 🐚🏹
🦄You are the mermaid unicorn. Drag your finger (or use the arrow keys) to swim wherever you want.
🏹Tap the sea or press the arrow button to shoot. Your arrows aim themselves!
🐚Swim over shells to collect them.
🍪The bag of cookies throws chocolate chips at you. Two arrows makes it pop!
😼The cat helps the cookies and chases you. One arrow sends it tumbling.
🥒The pickle is your friend — it squirts pickle juice at the cookies all by itself.
🐬The purple dolphin helps you: it fetches shells, bumps chocolate chips away, and gives you speed boosts.
🌀Swim into a tunnel to whoosh across the ocean!
💗Nobody can lose. If you get bonked you just spin around and drop a few shells — pick them back up!
